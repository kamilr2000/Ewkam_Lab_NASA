# S01 — Challenge description

Source: [2026 challenge page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/permanent-moon-base-locations-and-mars/?tab=details). Full read: 14 November 2026.

Title: **Identify Earth Locations that Analog the Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars**.

## Summary

The challenge asks teams to find and characterize Earth analogues for future Moon or Mars exploration locations using open Earth and planetary data, including NASA and other agency sources. Teams may study one location or search systematically across the world.

Similarity may involve geology, materials, terrain, temperature, ice, dust, biological processes, subsurface conditions, or operational factors. Similar appearance alone is insufficient. A useful analogue explains both similarities and differences and how the comparison could support exploration preparation. No single location is a perfect analogue for every condition.

The organizers encourage exploration beyond familiar analogue sites, explicit scale, and communication through maps, applications, reports, or other formats.

## Project interpretation

Layered criteria and MCDA are a product choice. Global coverage and a Top 10 are not challenge requirements. The project must show evidence, limits, and a possible use of the comparison. A candidate that scores well is a hypothesis for further verification, not a newly confirmed analogue.
